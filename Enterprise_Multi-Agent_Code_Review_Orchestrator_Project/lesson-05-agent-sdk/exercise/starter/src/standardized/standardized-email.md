### Risk Assessment

**🚨 CRITICAL MISSING TERMS:**
1. **No formal contract structure** - This is an informal email, not a legally binding contract
2. **Customer identity unclear** - "Team" is not a legal entity name
3. **No effective date** - When does the agreement begin?
4. **Undefined scope** - "Website redesign services" lacks detail on deliverables, pages, features, revisions
5. **No liability protection** - Missing limitation of liability clause
6. **No termination rights** - No terms for either party to exit the agreement
7. **IP ownership undefined** - Unclear who owns the designed website and assets
8. **No confidentiality terms** - No protection for sensitive business information
9. **No warranties or representations** - No service level guarantees
10. **Unclear completion criteria** - What defines "completion" for final payment?
11. **No dispute resolution** - Missing arbitration/mediation/jurisdiction clauses
12. **No late payment terms** - No interest or penalties specified
13. **No change order process** - How are scope changes handled?
14. **Missing governing law** - No jurisdiction specified
15. **No force majeure clause** - No protection for unforeseen circumstances

**⚠️ MEDIUM RISK ITEMS:**
- Payment structure is clear but lacks payment method details
- Timeline exists but no milestones or checkpoints defined
- Informal communication style may indicate lack of legal review

**RECOMMENDATION:**
This informal email should NOT be used as a contract. A formal written agreement should be drafted including all standard contract provisions, clear scope of work, deliverables, acceptance criteria, and legal protections for both parties.
